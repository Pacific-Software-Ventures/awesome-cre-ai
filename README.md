Maintained by [Pacific Software Ventures](https://pacificsoftwareventures.com) (PSV), an AI consulting and engineering firm for commercial real estate. PSV's own products and services are listed first in each section and marked "PSV's own". Every other entry is described from what that company publishes about itself; check details on their sites. ## Free resources and data

- [CRE calculators](https://pacificsoftwareventures.com/commercial-real-estate-calculators) (PSV's own): cap rate, NOI, DSCR, debt yield, mortgage and cash-on-cash calculators plus T-12, rent roll and OM analyzers; free, in the browser, no sign-up.
- [US Data Center Moratorium Tracker](https://pacificsoftwareventures.com/data-center-moratorium-tracker) (PSV's own): state and local moratoriums, permit pauses and large-load power rules, each row sourced. Dataset: [GitHub](https://github.com/Pacific-Software-Ventures/us-data-center-moratorium-tracker), [DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24325737](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24325737) (CC BY 4.0).
- [What AI works in commercial real estate](https://pacificsoftwareventures.com/what-ai-works-in-commercial-real-estate) (PSV's own): a scorecard of everyday CRE jobs graded works today, works with review, or not ready.
- [The CRE AI Consultant Landscape 2026](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24323438) (PSV's own): report comparing CRE AI consultancies on seven published criteria.
